## 1. Contract and completion standard

Adapt incoming firmware B to the physical drive running installed OEM A. Preserve A's hardware-dependent initialization, actuation, inputs, fault policy, calibration interpretation, persistent schema and ordinary save/recovery behavior. Preserve B's logical caller interfaces and nonhardware features. Replacing a complete physical driver is permitted when its API, imports, outputs, state, interrupts, timing, lifetime and placement are bound. Suppressing a fault flag or copying all of A and relabeling it B is not a correction.

Only **Normal and Kernel firmware components** may change. Existing drive data may be read through a proved read-only native interface. No conversion step writes, erases, migrates, formats, relocates or temporarily replaces parameter/calibration/NVRAM/bank data. Ordinary future OEM saves must preserve the installed representation and meaning. A firmware transfer must have a proved erase footprint disjoint from retained data; calling it “Normal” is insufficient.

A positive verdict states a detected domain and a complete constructive transformation. A negative verdict states the exact contradiction under the component/data constraints. A failed shortcut is not proof every possible correction fails. Demonstrably missing required component authority is a completed release0.18.0 negative for its exact predicate under the owner amendment, not physical impossibility. Unfinished internal design remains open. “All/some/none” must name its denominator and direction. Green Correctable describes implemented support whose full predicate succeeds; any pair outside that predicate refuses.

The evidence levels are distinct:

1. Native observation and exact component authority.
2. Complete behavior-preserving native correction and ownership.
3. Composition, original codecs, integrity and rebuilt authentication.
4. Actual application/receiver admission and every emitted intermediate stage.
5. Final-pinned route measurement and final dev CI.

None substitutes for the next. These are software evidence levels, not a claim that hardware has been physically qualified. Recovery's acknowledged family override remains as designed; it does not fabricate missing correction authority or waive mandatory data/transfer prerequisites. Do not reopen the solved44-byte/DSP work. UHD1959/1939 remain deferred. DEV only, no QA.
